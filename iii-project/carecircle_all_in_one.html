The Four Pillars of the Predictive Pipeline

Transitioning blood support from a reactive panic messaging loop into a predictive, automated standby network.

01

Circle 01: Predict

AI models patient transfusion cycles (e.g., Thalassemia on a 21-day recurrence) to schedule blood requirements 10 days out, long before panic sets in.

02

Circle 02: Prepare

Filters donor registry for compatibility, verifies eligibility cooldowns, and pre-warms secondary backup networks to secure standby responses.

03

Circle 03: Coordinate

Automates tracking confirmations via voice calling, establishes dispatch routes, and activates field volunteer couriers in real-time.

04

Circle 04: Prevent

Monitors matching timelines continuously. If primary networks fail, the system instantly triggers backup registries to prevent critical stockouts.
